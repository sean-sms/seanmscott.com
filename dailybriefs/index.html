The Daily Briefs

Personal tools by Sean Scott.

Each morning, a script on one Mac looks at the previous day's uploads from a short list of public YouTube channels, picks the best videos, and places them in private playlists in its owner's YouTube account.

Sports Daily Brief

Game highlights, big finishes, and top plays from the previous day.

News Daily Brief

The day's top stories, chosen by how widely established news outlets covered them.

These are not public services. There is no sign-up, and they are used only by their owner.
